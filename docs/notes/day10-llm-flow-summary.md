# LLM-written flow summary (llama3.1:8b, from the MCP walkthrough digest)

Here are the main user flows a customer can perform in plain language:

* **Registration**: A customer can register for a new account by filling out a form with their personal information and submitting it.
* **Login**: A customer can log in to their existing account by entering their username and password.
* **Open New Account**: A customer can open a new account by selecting the type of account they want to open and transferring funds into it.
* **Transfer Funds**: A customer can transfer funds from one account to another by selecting the accounts and entering the transfer amount.
* **Bill Pay**: A customer can pay bills by entering the payee information and sending the payment.
* **Find Transactions**: A customer can find transactions by date range by filling out a form with the desired date range.
* **Update Contact Info**: A customer can update their contact information by filling out a form with their new information.
* **Request Loan**: A customer can apply for a loan by filling out a form with their loan details.

Note: The digest does not provide clear information on how to navigate between these flows, so it is assumed that the customer can navigate between them by clicking on the relevant buttons or links.
