## Summary
The page does not refuse non-numeric amounts as expected. A payment request is sent with a non-numeric amount.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Log in to the ParaBank website
2. Go to the Bill Pay page
3. Enter a non-numeric amount in the Amount field
4. Click Send Payment

## Expected
The page should refuse to send a payment request with a non-numeric amount.

## Actual
A payment request is sent with a non-numeric amount.

## Evidence (checked: each quote occurs in the failure)
- `Amount: $ abc Please enter a valid amount.`
- `a rejected form sent a payment request: ['http://localhost:8080/parabank/services_proxy/bank/billpay?accountId=0&amount=1']`

## Suspected cause
The page's validation logic may not be correctly implemented.

## Next action
The development team should review the page's validation logic to ensure it correctly refuses non-numeric amounts.
