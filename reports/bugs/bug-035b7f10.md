1. Open the ParaBank website and navigate to the Transfer Funds page
2. Enter a decimal amount in the transfer field
3. Wait for the page to timeout

## Expected
The amount field should be visible and the transfer should be successful

## Actual

## Evidence (checked: each quote occurs in the failure)

## Suspected cause
The browser or application is taking too long to load the amount field
