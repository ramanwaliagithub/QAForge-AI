# Transfer funds
As a logged-in customer, I want to transfer money between my own accounts so that I can move funds without visiting a branch.

## Acceptance criteria
- The Transfer Funds page asks for an amount, a "from" account and a "to" account.
- A successful transfer shows "Transfer Complete!" with the amount and both account ids.
- The amount must be a positive number.
- The "from" and "to" accounts are chosen from the customer's own accounts.
