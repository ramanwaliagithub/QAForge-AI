# Open new account
As a logged-in customer, I want to open a new checking or savings account so that I can organise my money.

## Acceptance criteria
- The Open New Account page offers an account type (CHECKING or SAVINGS) and a funding account.
- Opening an account requires an initial deposit transferred from the selected funding account.
- A successful request shows "Account Opened!" with the new account number.
- The new account appears in Accounts Overview.
